I'm an applied microeconomist studying labour market integration and human capital formation. Broadly, my research interests lie within labour and public economics, including topics in the fields of economics of migration, education, health, and crime. My current research examines how institutional structures and policy design influence integration and welfare outcomes among newly arrived immigrants in Sweden, using quasi-experimental variation arising from administrative procedures.

My current projects have received the required ethical approval and research funding. This page will be updated with more details once relevant progress has been made.
